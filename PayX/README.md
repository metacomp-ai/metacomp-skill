# PayX

**Payment orchestration layer connecting fiat and digital rails — for global merchant collections and payouts.**

---

## What is PayX?

PayX is MetaComp's payment orchestration engine. It abstracts the complexity of cross-border payments into a single, unified layer — routing transactions intelligently across fiat networks and digital asset rails, settling in real time, and staying continuously aligned with regulatory requirements.

For merchants, PayX means accepting payments from anywhere and settling in the currency and format they prefer. For platforms and enterprises, it means embedding global payment capability without building the compliance infrastructure from scratch.

---

## How It Works

PayX runs on two integrated engines:

### StableX
The settlement engine. StableX handles the conversion, routing, and final delivery of value across fiat and stablecoin rails — optimizing for speed, cost, and liquidity. It enables real-time settlement where traditional correspondent banking would take days.

### VisionX
The compliance engine. Every payment flowing through PayX is evaluated by VisionX in real time — screening counterparties, flagging risk, and ensuring the transaction meets the regulatory standards of the relevant jurisdiction before it moves.

Together, they form a closed loop: **move fast, stay clean.**

---

## Capabilities

- **Global collections** — accept payments from customers worldwide across local fiat rails and digital assets
- **Cross-border payouts** — disburse to merchants, partners, or end users in their preferred currency and network
- **Real-time settlement** — eliminate the float and delays of traditional payment infrastructure
- **Built-in compliance** — every transaction screened against KYT, sanctions, and regulatory rules automatically
- **Unified API** — one integration covers the full payment lifecycle: initiate, route, settle, report

---

## Regulatory Foundation

PayX is built on MetaComp's **Major Payment Institution (MPI) license**, issued by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). This isn't compliance as an afterthought — it is the foundation the product is built on. Merchants and platforms that integrate PayX inherit that regulatory posture.
