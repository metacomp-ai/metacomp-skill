# WealthX

**MetaComp's treasury and wealth management platform — turning idle capital into active yield, across Web2 and Web3.**

---

## What is WealthX?

Every enterprise and payment service provider sits on pools of capital that aren't working. Float between collections and payouts. Operational reserves. Settlement buffers. In traditional finance, this money earns little or nothing — locked in accounts that prioritize accessibility over returns.

WealthX changes that equation. It is a treasury and wealth management platform that connects businesses to a curated range of yield opportunities — from tokenized money market funds and real-world assets to on-chain digital yield products — and generates returns on an **hourly basis**. Every dollar that would otherwise sit idle is put to work, continuously.

---

## The Yield Stack

WealthX bridges Web2 and Web3 to offer a complete spectrum of treasury products:

| Product | Type | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Tokenized MMF** | Web2 → Web3 | Capital-preservation focus, institutional-grade liquidity, on-chain accessibility |
| **RWA (Real-World Assets)** | Web2 → Web3 | Yield backed by real-world instruments — bonds, receivables, structured credit |
| **Digital Yield** | Web3 native | On-chain yield strategies with real-time transparency and flexible tenors |

Enterprises can allocate across the stack based on their own liquidity needs, risk appetite, and return targets — or let WealthX optimize the mix automatically.

---

## Hourly Yield — Why It Matters

Traditional treasury products compound daily at best. WealthX generates yield on an **hourly basis**, which changes the economics of short-term capital deployment fundamentally:

- A PSP holding $10M in settlement float earns yield from the moment funds arrive — not the next banking day
- Intraday cash released from a completed settlement cycle goes straight to work instead of sitting overnight
- Working capital efficiency improves because the cost of holding liquidity drops

For high-volume payment businesses, the difference between hourly and daily compounding at scale is not marginal — it is material.

## Built For

**Enterprises** — treasury teams looking to optimize returns on operational cash and reserves without taking on undue risk or sacrificing liquidity.

**Payment Service Providers (PSPs)** — platforms with significant float that want to monetize idle balances between collections and payouts, improving unit economics across their payment flows.

Both use cases are served through a single platform, with the compliance and custody infrastructure already built in.

---

## Role in the MetaComp Ecosystem

WealthX sits alongside MetaComp's payment and compliance infrastructure as the capital optimization layer:

- **PayX** settlement flows feed directly into WealthX — funds in transit can be put to work during the settlement window
- **StableX** handles the conversion legs when moving between fiat reserves and yield-bearing digital products
- **VisionX** ensures all counterparties and instruments meet compliance requirements before capital is deployed
