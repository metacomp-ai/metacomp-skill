# VisionX

**MetaComp's integrated compliance intelligence engine — unifying Web2 and Web3 risk into a single view.**

---

## What is VisionX?

The compliance gap between traditional finance and digital assets is one of the most consequential unsolved problems in fintech. Banks see fiat flows. On-chain analytics tools see blockchain activity. Neither sees the full picture — and that blind spot is exactly where risk hides.

VisionX is MetaComp's answer. Built as a **Web 2.5 risk engine**, it sits at the intersection of traditional finance and digital asset ecosystems, pulling together KYC identity data, fiat transaction history, and on-chain activity into a single, unified risk profile for every entity it monitors.

The result is compliance intelligence that neither world could produce alone.

---

## The Web 2.5 Risk Model

Most compliance tools are built for one world or the other. VisionX is built for the space in between:

| Layer | Data Sources | What It Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| **Web2 (Fiat)** | KYC records, bank transactions, payment history | Identity, behavioral patterns, fiat exposure |
| **Web3 (On-chain)** | Wallet activity, transaction graphs, protocol interactions | Asset flows, counterparty risk, on-chain exposure |
| **Web 2.5 (Unified)** | Cross-domain correlation | The full risk picture — who they are, what they do, where the money moves |

When a payment entity has a clean KYC record but routes funds through high-risk wallets on-chain, only a Web 2.5 engine catches it. That's VisionX.

## Capabilities

**Unified Risk Profiles**
Every entity — individual, merchant, or counterparty — gets a consolidated risk score that reflects their complete financial footprint across fiat and crypto. No more siloed assessments.

**AML Monitoring**
Continuous, cross-domain AML surveillance that tracks behavioral patterns over time. VisionX detects structuring, layering, and integration across both traditional and digital rails — not just point-in-time snapshots.

**Real-Time Transaction Screening**
Every transaction is screened before it moves. VisionX evaluates counterparty risk, sanctions exposure, and on-chain provenance in real time — enabling compliant transactions at speed, rather than blocking them after the fact.

**KYT (Know Your Transaction)**
Deep transaction-level intelligence for both fiat and crypto flows. For Web3, VisionX aggregates signals from multiple on-chain analytics vendors to provide cross-validated risk assessments — not single-source verdicts.
